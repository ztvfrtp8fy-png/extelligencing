# How We Prioritize

Light and evidence-aware, no committee.

1. **Collect.** Issues and Discussions. Feedback is voluntary and should never include private content.
2. **Label** each item: `bug`, `friction` (unwanted intervention or setup pain), `context` (retrieval problems), `runtime` (adapter or live-verification reports), `workspace` (shared-workspace needs), `cost` (credits, tokens, latency), `idea`.
3. **Weigh by evidence**, roughly in this order:
   - Reproducible harm or data-safety issue
   - Reports from several roles or runtimes
   - Reports with a concrete example or measurement
   - Single opinions
4. **Prefer removal and simplification** to additions. Core text costs context on every session.
5. **Review** monthly while in beta. Move items to the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) only with a stated problem, who has it, and how we will know it is solved.
6. **Keep internal and public feedback apart.** Organization-specific feedback stays in that organization; only generic, synthetic-safe learnings come into the public project.
