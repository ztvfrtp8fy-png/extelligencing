# Code of Conduct

Extelligencing is for professionals with many backgrounds, roles and levels of experience. We want participation to be respectful, useful and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give specific, actionable feedback. Accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect privacy. Never share other people's personal data or your employer's confidential information.
- Be honest about evidence. Say what you tested, and what you did not.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats or sexualized attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without consent.
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or manipulating votes and engagement.
- Sharing proprietary or confidential material you do not have the right to share.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions, and may restrict participation, when behavior breaks these expectations. To report a concern, use GitHub's private reporting for this repository, or contact a maintainer through the channel listed in `SECURITY.md`. Reports are handled with discretion.

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public.
